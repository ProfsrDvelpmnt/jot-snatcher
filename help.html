How to Use JOT Snatcher

Collecting A Job

Collecting Job Information

  • • Navigate to any supported job site (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)
  • • Find a job posting you're interested in
  • • Click the JOT Snatcher extension icon in your browser
  • • Click the "Collect" button in the popup
  • • Wait for the extraction to complete (usually 2-3 seconds)
  • • Review the collected job data in the Details tab

Troubleshooting

Common issues and their solutions

Extension Not Collecting Job Data

  • Make sure you're on a supported job site (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)
  • Refresh the page and try again
  • Check if you're logged into your SP-JOT account
  • Verify you haven't exceeded your monthly job limit
  • Try disabling and re-enabling the extension

Can't Sign In

  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify your SP-JOT account credentials
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Try signing in through the SP-JOT website first
  • Contact support if the issue persists

Usage Limit Reached

  • You've reached your monthly job collection limit
  • Check the Status tab to see your current usage
  • Consider upgrading your subscription plan
  • Your limit will reset at the beginning of next month
  • Contact support for plan upgrade assistance

Can't Send Job Data

  • Ensure you have an active internet connection
  • Check that you're logged into your SP-JOT account
  • Verify your subscription is active
  • Try refreshing the popup and sending again
  • Check the Options page for API configuration

General Tips - Best Practices

  • Keep the extension updated to the latest version
  • Use supported job sites for best results
  • Check your usage regularly to avoid limits
  • Export important jobs immediately

Performance Tips

  • Close unused browser tabs for better performance
  • Clear browser cache periodically
  • Disable other extensions if experiencing issues
  • Restart browser if extension becomes unresponsive
